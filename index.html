Persistance of productivity shocks in stochastic growth models

An analysis of the effects of changing persistance in productivity shocks on simple stochastic growth models with borrowing constraints. This project is particularly interested in the impact of volatility/persistance on the binding of the capital constraint and probabilistic distribution of the capital stock for the representative agent.
